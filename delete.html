Delete your data

SayWhat AI · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This page is for the SayWhat AI app. SayWhat AI has no account and no server, so the developer does not keep a copy of your data and there is nothing to send a deletion request to. Data the app stored is only on your device. You delete it yourself, in the app. Deletion on the device is immediate. SayWhat AI keeps nothing after that.

Steps

  1. Conversation reports only. On Home, tap the history icon. On Session History, tap Clear, then Delete. Transcripts and feedback for those sessions are removed from this device. Your API key, name and settings stay.
  2. Gemini API key only. Open Profile, tap Gemini API Key, then Remove. The key is removed from this device immediately. The key in Google AI Studio is not deleted by this step.
  3. Everything the app stored. Open Profile, tap Reset app data, then Erase everything. This immediately deletes the API key, your name, photo or avatar, level, tutor, learning goal, streak, XP, saved conversations and feedback, words, pronunciation scores and grammar flags. It cannot be undone.
  4. Uninstall. Removing SayWhat AI from the device also deletes the app's local storage.

What is deleted, and what is kept

The steps above delete data stored by SayWhat AI on that device. They do not delete audio or text already sent to Google's Gemini API. SayWhat AI never received that data and cannot delete Google's copy. How long Google keeps it is covered by the Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy. SayWhat AI does not delete that data within 90 days, because it is not held by SayWhat AI.

SayWhat AI does not retain data on a server after you delete it on the device. There is no extra retention period, because there is no server copy.

The full privacy policy is at https://kanzapro71.github.io/saywhat-ai-privacy/.
